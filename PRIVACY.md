# Privacy Policy — super fair odds

**Sunday Labs · Effective November 6, 2026**

---

## This app does not collect your data

super fair odds collects no personal information, sends no data to any server, and contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind. Everything the app does happens entirely on your device. There is no account to create, no sign-in required, and nothing stored anywhere other than your own phone.

---

## What we collect

Nothing. super fair odds does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information or usage data. This includes:

- No names, email addresses, or contact details
- No device identifiers or advertising IDs
- No location data
- No usage statistics or analytics
- No crash reports sent off-device
- No cookies or similar tracking technologies

---

## Third-party services

super fair odds does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or data-sharing services. The app makes no network requests.

---

## Children's privacy

Because super fair odds collects no data from anyone, it is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children or anyone else.
